# Wellness Flow

https://wellnessmedclub.com/


Wellness Med Club – Patient Workflow, Ordering System & Compliance Model





At Wellness Med Club, I have created a streamlined, compliant, and patient-centered system that allows individuals to easily select and purchase supplements, peptides, lab panels, and medications while ensuring all medical, legal, and safety requirements are met.








1. Online Product Selection & Checkout





Patients can browse and select products directly through my platform, including:



Vitamins and supplements
Peptides and injectables
Lab panels
Hormone and prescription-based therapies




Each product includes a clear description, intended use, and any necessary precautions.



Once a patient selects a product:



They add it to their cart
Proceed to checkout
Complete all required intake forms and consents prior to finalizing their order









2. Required Intake & Eligibility Screening





Before any purchase is completed, every patient must fill out a brief medical intake questionnaire to ensure safety and protect my practice.



This includes:



Confirmation of residency in Florida or Nevada
Basic health screening (yes/no format)
Current medications and supplements
Allergies
Pregnancy or breastfeeding status
Relevant medical history (cardiac, endocrine, etc.)




Patients must attest that all information provided is accurate and truthful.








3. Product-Specific Consents & Acknowledgments





Each product category has a targeted consent form that directly correlates with what the patient is ordering.





General Medical Disclaimer





Patients acknowledge risks, benefits, and limitations
Agreement that participation is voluntary
Liability acknowledgment protecting the provider




Vitamin & Supplement Consent



Patients confirm they have had lab work within the past year
They acknowledge they are either deficient or seeking optimization
This supports patient autonomy while reducing unnecessary clinical burden


Peptides & Injectable Consent



Education on proper use and administration
Risks, benefits, and potential side effects
Agreement to follow usage guidelines




All consents must be electronically signed prior to checkout completion.








4. Lab Panel Ordering & Processing Policy





Patients may purchase lab panels directly through the platform, including my structured seven-panel lab option.



At checkout, patients must sign an acknowledgment confirming:



Understanding of the selected lab panel
That results require clinical correlation
That processing times apply






Processing Timeline





Lab orders are processed within 24 business hours
Orders placed:
On Fridays may be processed the next business day (Monday)
Weekends and holidays are excluded from processing time



5. Lab Requirements for Hormone & Controlled Therapies





For hormone-based and controlled therapies (such as testosterone), stricter clinical requirements are enforced.



Patients must:



Have completed relevant lab work within the last 6 months




If lab work is not available:



Patients are required to purchase and complete appropriate lab testing through the platform
No treatment will be initiated without current labs


6. Controlled Substances & Consultation Requirement



For any controlled or prescription-based therapies:



A mandatory 15-minute consultation with me is required
This consultation is included in the total cost




During checkout:



Patients are prompted to schedule their consultation immediately




Patients may proceed with checkout; however:



The order is placed in a pending status
No prescription will be written or released until:
Required lab work is completed and reviewed
The consultation has been completed
I determine medical appropriateness



7. Integrated Scheduling & EMR System





Scheduling is integrated directly with my EMR, SimplePractice.



Patients click “Book Appointment” during checkout
They are redirected to my live scheduling calendar
Available appointments are shown in real time
Once booked, the visit is automatically documented in their chart

This allows for:
Seamless scheduling
Accurate documentation
Improved workflow efficiency
